A few practical questions.

Can I buy a plan today?

Not yet. Plans open at launch. Until then you can explore the full sample workspace, as a studio or as a client, with no account.

Will clients need a paid seat?

No. Clients review, approve and message the studio free of charge.

What is the annual saving?

A yearly plan costs ten monthly payments: £290, £590 or £990 per year, paid annually. That is approximately 16.7% below twelve monthly payments.
