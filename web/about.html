Why Caleane

Make room
for the actual work.

A creative project is shaped by people: what they need, what they see and what they decide. Those conversations deserve a place beside the work.

A workspace for creatives

Keep the project
in the conversation.

The brief in one thread. Feedback in another. A new version somewhere else. Caleane brings those parts into one considered workspace for the studio and its client.

From the first enquiry to a shared brief, a design review and an agreed direction, the people and the context stay with the project.
