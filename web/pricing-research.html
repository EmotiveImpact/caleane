Commercial proposal / 1 October 2026

A considered starting price.

Recommendation: £29, £59 and £99 per studio per month. Test willingness to pay before treating these as final launch prices.

The comparison

ProductMonthly billing, USDCharging model
Dubsado$35 Starter / $55 PremierPlan based
HoneyBook$36 Starter / $59 Essentials / $129 PremiumPlan based
Bonsai$25 Essentials / $39 Premium / $59 ElitePer user; Elite has a minimum

Prices checked on official product and help pages. Competitor figures are USD; the Caleane proposal is GBP. These are positioning references, not currency-converted equivalents. Promotions are excluded.

Why this structure

£29 gives an independent a credible entry point. £59 supports a five-person studio without charging five separate subscriptions. £99 gives a ten-person practice a clear upper tier. Keep review and approval in every plan: that is the product’s distinguishing workflow.

What must be validated

  • Image storage, delivery and support costs per active studio.
  • Willingness to pay from independents and small teams.
  • Usage limits and additional-seat economics.
  • Tax treatment, trial length and cancellation terms.

At the proposed monthly prices, 100 Studio subscribers represent £5,900 of monthly subscription revenue before taxes, fees, infrastructure and support. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.
