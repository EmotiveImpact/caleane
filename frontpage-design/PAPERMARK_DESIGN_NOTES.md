# What the Papermark page teaches Caleane

Reference reviewed: https://www.papermark.com/ on 29 September 2026.

## Visual lessons applied

1. **Let the hero hold attention.** A single full-frame portrait, an outcome-led line, and very little navigation beat a crowded collage. Caleane keeps its own portrait and lowers the headline into the photograph's open space.
2. **Show the product while interest is high.** Papermark places a large product visual just after the opening. Caleane now moves its sample workspace directly after the black problem statement, before its process and profession stories.
3. **Make the visual example explain the promise.** The sample workspace shows one brief revision, one design version with pinned feedback, and the resulting creative decision. The tabs are stages in one project, rather than disconnected decorative screens.
4. **Change visual tempo deliberately.** The sequence is portrait, black statement, product proof over a working-surface photograph, short paper index, immersive photographs, then a photographed invitation. The three-step index links directly to the matching scenes.
5. **Keep proof honest.** Papermark can show customers, metrics, case studies and security credentials. Caleane uses labelled sample content and concept photography until it has corresponding real evidence.

## Further design work when product evidence exists

- Replace the illustrative project frame with a crisp capture from the live product once its visual language is final.
- Add actual studio case studies or outcomes with permission; make them image-led and specific.
- Add a real trust or security section only when its claims can be verified.
