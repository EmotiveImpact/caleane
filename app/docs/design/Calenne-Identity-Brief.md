# Calenne identity and production brief

30 September 2026. Recommended direction: Shared Frame. Recommendation for review, not an approved identity or trademark clearance.

## Brand character

Calenne is attentive, visually literate, composed and dependable. It should feel at home in a studio and sufficiently clear for its clients. Its promise is continuity: from first enquiry to an approved direction, the brief, references, versions and decisions stay in view. Let people and their creative work supply the emotion; the identity supplies recognition and order.

Each generated board shows positive and reversed marks, illustrative favicon samples, a homepage header with editorial portrait, a compact sidebar, an app icon and a project document or folder. These are conceptual raster images. Font rendering, pixel dimensions and symbol proportions within them are not authoritative production specifications. No local Calenne site was inspected. Papermark's accessible page content was used for navigation/footer context, not copied as a logo or claimed as a visually inspected screenshot.

## Recommendation

Choose Shared Frame with the title-case Calenne wordmark. The two corners suggest keeping an entire direction in view without choosing a single profession's tool as the emblem. Its solid silhouette survives software sizes, and its open centre gives it breathing room beside intimate portraits. The softer wordmark balances the angular symbol. Drop the final period: the symbol already provides a recognisable punctuation-like element, and the name needs no further ornament. Use Calenne consistently in interface and prose. Uppercase may be used for small editorial labels, never as a second competing logo.

Use green-black on sage. Do not use sage for small text on ivory. Keep success, pending and error colours separate from the identity; colour alone must not communicate approval. No gradient logo, outlines, emboss effects in the UI, or automatic recolouring to match each portrait. A physical deboss is an optional material application, not the master logo.

## Typography

Keep Instrument Sans across the site and app, upright only, at its normal width. Start the wordmark at weight 600. Retain the default double-storey a and ordinary e; do not invent a decorative letter or substitute a different font to make the name appear bespoke. The final distinctiveness is in the symbol and the fixed lettering arrangement.

Use 400 for body, 500 for navigation and interface labels, and 500 or 600 for headings. Product text: 14–16 px with 20–24 px line height; supporting marketing text: 18 px with 27 px line height; desktop hero: approximately 56–72 px with 1.05–1.12 line height, adjusted to fit real viewports. Use tabular numerals for numerical version lists where useful. Keep all copy free of decorative link arrows and italic emphasis.

Instrument Sans's official repository identifies the family as SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans . Licence: https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans/blob/master/OFL.txt . It can be used in commercial web/app work under the licence. Retain its copyright/licence notices when distributing the font. Outlining a logo does not make the typeface proprietary. Do not claim exclusivity over the font or redistribute a modified font without checking the licence conditions. The package does not include font binaries.

## Precise symbol construction

The included SVG geometry is the authoritative proposed symbol, not an automatic trace of generated imagery.

Use a 96 × 96 unit square. Upper-left solid polygon vertices: (0,0), (52,0), (52,22), (22,22), (22,52), (0,52). Lower-right solid polygon vertices: (96,96), (44,96), (44,74), (74,74), (74,44), (96,44). The second is a 180-degree rotation of the first about (48,48). Use filled paths, square corners, no strokes, no clipping mask. The 22-unit thickness is fixed. Never close the frame or add the other two corners.

The supplied 16px and 32px favicon PNGs use an intentionally pixel-aligned small-size variant. At 16px, green-black background, ivory upper-left L vertices (2,2), (8,2), (8,5), (5,5), (5,8), (2,8); the other L is its rotation about (8,8). At 32px double those coordinates. Do not resize the 16px file to make large marketing artwork. Use the master SVG for that.

## Wordmark and lockup production

Set exactly Calenne, with no period, in Instrument Sans 600, normal width, upright. Begin with the font's native kerning, then optically review Ca, al, ll, le, en and ne at 24px, 32px and 120px. Fix the chosen positions in a master; do not set a global negative tracking value as a substitute for pair review. Keep default letter shapes and equal l stem heights. Supply both live-type source and an outlined master after review. No wordmark vector is included because the generated lettering is not an accurate font master.

For a horizontal lockup, let H equal the outlined wordmark's capital-C height. Symbol height: 1.10H. Gap from symbol's rightmost edge to C's leftmost visible edge: 0.45H. Centre the symbol on the wordmark's visible vertical bounds; make only a small optical adjustment after side-by-side testing. Clear space on every side: at least 0.50H. Wordmark-only is allowed where the full identity is already visible elsewhere. Symbol-only is for collapsed navigation, app icons and favicons.

Provisional minimum sizes: full lockup 120px wide on screen or 28mm in print; wordmark-only 88px wide or 22mm; symbol-only 20px or 5mm. The dedicated favicon is the exception at 16px. These are starting specifications, not verified wordmark legibility thresholds. Increase size if the tested font master loses clarity. Test on ordinary and high-density displays before release.

## Applications

Homepage header: use a 24–28px symbol beside a wordmark approximately 22–26px high. Keep 32–48px desktop inset and 20–24px mobile inset; adapt to the actual page grid. White lockup over a reliably dark part of the image. Check every portrait and intermediate crossfade, not just the first image. Use a restrained dark overlay where needed. Keep the mark away from eyes and faces. Never animate or morph the logo during portrait crossfades.

Sidebar: expanded state uses the full lockup in a 28–32px high brand row. Collapsed state uses the symbol alone at 24px; make its clickable area at least 44px square with an accessible name. Do not turn the brand frame into an approval/status indicator.

App icon: green-black or sage field, flat single-colour symbol, approximately 58% of the field width, centred. Supply square source artwork; operating-system masks are applied separately. Do not bake a shadow or a rounded device mask into the source.

Document: green-black mark on ivory; one quiet heading such as Direction review; align to the same left margin. Use white on green-black for a dark cover. Photography may sit below the title, but must not fill or texture the symbol.

Footer: carry the small wordmark once, then clearly grouped Product, Studios, Resources, Company and Legal navigation. Only include real destinations. Papermark is structural context for comprehensive SaaS navigation, not a source of Calenne claims or brand assets.

## Included artwork and release checks

Included: editable symbol SVGs in green-black and ivory; square app-icon SVGs; pixel-aligned 16px and 32px favicon PNGs; this brief. These are development assets for the recommended geometry. They are not a complete approved logo suite. Generated boards remain concept studies. No EPS, AI or outlined wordmark is claimed.

Before release: optically finish and outline the wordmark; inspect 16/20/24/32px symbols and the actual header/sidebar; verify colour/contrast through all portrait transitions; check ordinary single-colour print and reversal; conduct appropriate mark/name similarity checks. Record the approved lockup rather than allowing developers to recreate its spacing.

Final production deliverables: editable master with separate symbol and wordmark components; outlined SVG and PDF horizontal lockups in positive/reverse; wordmark-only and symbol-only files; transparent PNGs; favicon.ico containing 16/32/48px variants; 180px touch icon; 192/512px app icons; colour/type tokens and a one-page usage sheet. Avoid re-tracing raster lettering.
